A game I keep
coming back to

I've played competitive chess for around eight years and still compete nationally and internationally. Over time, chess also became more than just playing tournaments — I've volunteered in the chess community, helped run events, and became a National Arbiter.

A couple of tournament highlights
  • Top 10, North American U16 Youth Chess Championship
  • Top 10, Pan-American School U17 Chess Championship

How I like to play

I usually enjoy sharp, attacking positions where one inaccurate move can turn the whole game around. At the same time, I don’t mind grinding out a positional game when the position calls for it.

My favorite opening as White is somehow still the London System—yes, I know. With Black, I’m much more at home in open Sicilian positions where things get messy quickly.

That's me, for now.
