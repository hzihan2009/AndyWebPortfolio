01

MCC Member App

The problem

Tournament registration and check-in used to rely heavily on manual work. On busy nights, things could get crowded and disorganized. Through volunteering, I saw that friction directly and wanted to make the process easier for players.

What I built

I built the MCC Member App so members can handle parts of registration and check-in before arriving, rather than doing everything at the club.

What members can do

  • Log in with their own account.
  • Register for the tournament or event they plan to compete in.
  • Check in online before arriving.
  • Access their club and member information.

Connected to the organizers

The app connects to the tournament administration system, so organizers can see who has registered and checked in.

02

MCC Tournament Administration System

The organizer-facing half of the same system. While members register and check in through the app, tournament organizers use this side to manage the tournament.

Running the tournament

Organizers can see which members are participating, manage registration and check-in status, and keep player information up to date. They can also manage or remove members when needed and track tournament participation.

Keeping things connected

The system supports pairing-related workflows, player tracking, and name-card management. Its connection to the member app gives organizers better visibility into who has registered or checked in.

Why it matters

The goal is less friction on tournament nights: members can handle parts of the process before arriving, and organizers have a clearer view of who's participating.
